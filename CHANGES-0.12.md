## New: the Pocket Dimension Machine (3x3x3 multiblock)
Three new blocks:
- **Pocket Casing** (needs 25): 4 Obsidian + 4 Polished Blackstone + 1 Netherite Ingot makes 4.
- **Pocket Controls** (1): Casing + Ender Eye + Comparator.
- **Pocket Core** (1): Casing + Ender Chest + 2 Echo Shards.

How to build it:
1. Build a 3x3x3 cube. The Core is in the middle.
2. The Controls go in the middle of ONE side (not the top or bottom).
3. The other 25 blocks are Casings.
4. Right-click the Controls. If the shape is right, the machine locks.

While locked, the 27 blocks cannot be broken (not even by explosions or pistons).
Open the Controls (right-click) for the menu:
- **Upgrade pocket space** (Starglass from your inventory)
- **Link a Gate**: press it, then right-click a Quantum Gate within 30 seconds
- **Take the machine apart**: unlocks the blocks. They can then be mined with a diamond pickaxe.

A linked Quantum Gate takes whoever uses it to the owner's pocket space.
So giving someone access means putting down a linked gate they can reach.
Inside the pocket there is a Gate that brings you back.
