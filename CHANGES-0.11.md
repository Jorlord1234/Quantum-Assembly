# What changed in 0.11

## Fixed
- The Cauldron screen no longer closes at once. Pick-block and Jade now show our Cauldron, not a normal pot.
- The Nebula Blaze Burner has its old (v0.9) blaze look back. Only the cage changed: it is now see-through.
  Normal Create burners are not touched by this mod.
- The Kit handle is smaller. The gem on the lid is now exactly in the middle.
- The Chef's Hat is the classic shape again: a very long middle part and a small puff on top.

## New
- The Quantum Assembly Kit opens a screen: 3 ritual slots + 36 storage slots.
  One slot holds up to 256 items (config: kitStackLimit). Breaking the Kit drops everything.
- The pocket dimension (first version):
  - The ritual now gives a Quantum Gate BLOCK. Place it and right-click it to go to YOUR OWN pocket space.
  - Your space is a floor in a void sky with invisible, unbreakable walls (barrier blocks).
  - Inside there is a Quantum Gate. Right-click it to come back where you left.
  - Right-click any Gate with Starglass to upgrade your space bigger (level 1 to 5):
    level 1 = 25 blocks wide (8 Starglass), 2 = 33 (16), 3 = 49 (32), 4 = 65 (64), 5 = 97 (128).
    The starting size (level 0) is 17 blocks wide.
  - Falling off brings you back to your space.
- New advancement: A Pocket Full of Stars.
