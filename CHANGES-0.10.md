# What changed in 0.10

## Fixed
- Nebula Blaze Burner has its own new look (purple frame and head, dark when cold).
- Catalyst on an Enchantment Industry burner (Blaze Enchanter and friends) now adds SUPER experience.
  It costs 1 bucket and the Catalyst is not used up. Normal Create burners are never touched.
- The Uncompleted Nova Coin now only finishes in THE END.
- Starglass Ore drops 1 to 4.
- Chef's Hat is now a very, very tall hat that you can really see on your head.
- Kit handle is now an arch like the Create Toolbox.

## New
- The Master Chef's Cauldron is now a real Farmer's Delight cooking pot.
- New cake recipe:
  1. Create press (compacting): Powdered Obsidian + Echo Shard + Nugget of Super Experience = Cake Base
  2. Spout: Cake Base + 250 mB Dragon's Breath = Nebula Batter
  3. Cook the Nebula Batter in the Master Chef's Cauldron on a heat source = Cake of Nebulae
- The Nebula Blaze Burner counts as a heat source for the cooking pot.
- 10 advancements ("Quantum Assembly" tab).

## Checked, nothing to change
- The Nova Coin's Waystones menu and free teleports match how Waystones 21.1.46 works.

## Not done yet (planned)
- The Kit as a big storage block (backpack + toolbox)
- The Quantum Gate block, the pocket dimension, upgrades, kinetic link
- The Gate is still a test item
